About This Page

These photos were taken on the last day of the semester to celebrate our graduation and the Graduation Exhibition Project (GEP) we built together in Arts, Literature & Communication. We came from different corners of the arts, and somehow ended up a team. Thank you to every classmate and teacher who made it feel that way.

Some of us are at university now, and some of us took a year off to figure out what comes next. We came in as writers, theatre performers, dancers, poets, filmmakers, and the lone computer science student, and whatever we came in with, this class gave us a place to grow. Wherever you are, I hope you keep writing, performing, filming, and making things.

- Andrew

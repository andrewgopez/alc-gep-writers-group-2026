The Group

Two from the GEP itself, taken the week before our last day, because one photo was never going to be enough.

GEP week, with our projects on display.

Nobody planned these, and that's why I like them. We'd spent the whole GEP in the same room, reading each other's drafts, giving feedback, and sharing the stress of deadlines. Somewhere along the way, we stopped being strangers. Some of us had known each other from other classes, and some of us had only just met, but by the time these were taken, it didn't matter who had known whom.

These photos caught that moment: everyone smiling at the camera, and nobody needing to be introduced. Looking at them now, that's what I remember most about this group.

It also made me realize that a program is only as good as the people in it. Our projects were the work, but you were the reason it felt like something worth doing. Thank you for every workshop, every honest comment, and every time someone made space for the quiet person in the back.

- Andrew
